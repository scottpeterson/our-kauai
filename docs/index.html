Dairy Queen and Grindz

Eleele Shopping Center

FoodClosed

What has stood here

  1. Dairy Queen
  2. Grindz
  3. Today: Gather Federal Credit Union

On the way home from Salt Pond, we stopped for ice cream here or at McDonald's, until Dairy Queen closed.

I think Matt once took everyone out to lunch at Grindz and paid with his own money. He was young, maybe 10 years old. Grandpa and Grandma both ordered the cheapest item, chili on rice, because they didn't want to spend much of Matt's money.

Salt Pond Beach Park

Hanapepe

BeachStill there
Photos from
2016, 2017, 2022

Every day around 11 a.m., we would make deli sandwiches, pack up, and head to Salt Pond. My younger brother Matt and I would boogie board while Mom, Grandpa, and Grandma sat on the beach in beach chairs and on bamboo mats. We would eat our sandwiches, then head home.

On the way home, we stopped for ice cream at Dairy Queen (until it closed) or McDonald's.
